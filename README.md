# GeoIntelligence AI

## AI-Based Geopolitical Conflict, Escalation & Economic Impact Forecasting

GeoIntelligence AI is an AI-powered platform that combines geopolitical, conflict, economic, energy, trade and news signals to estimate changing conflict and escalation risk and explore potential economic impacts.

## Core Features

- Conflict Risk Prediction
- Early Warning System
- Escalation Forecasting
- Country Relationship Network
- Interactive Geopolitical Map
- Regional Ripple Effect Analysis
- News & NLP Intelligence
- Oil Price Forecasting
- Shipping Risk Indicators
- Economic Impact Forecasting
- What-If Geopolitical Simulator
- Explainable AI using SHAP
- Multiple Forecast Horizons
